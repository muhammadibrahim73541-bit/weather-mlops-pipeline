**English Poem:**

In scorching lands and coastal breeze,
Three cities stood, with weather's ease.

Aalborg, cool and by the sea,
27.2°C, a temperature to be.
But at night, a chill sets in fast,
26.3 winds that dance, in a gentle cast.

Doha's heat, a burning flame,
39.6°C, a temperature to claim.
The desert sun, a burning test,
0.0 rain, a desert's unrest.

Lahore's heat, a gentle hue,
37.9°C, a temperature anew.
The winds are still, but rain falls light,
0.2 drops, on a summer's night.

But which one's best, when summer's here?
Aalborg's breeze, brings relief so clear.
Cool and calm, it's a perfect blend,
Aalborg's the place, to weather end.
