**English Poem:**

Cities three, so different and grand,
Lahore, Doha, Aalborg, each in its land.
Weather they share, but distinct they stand,
Each with its charm, and its own weather band.

Aalborg's cool breeze whispers through the trees,
A gentle 15 degrees, for calm it pleases.
Doha's heat waves dance, a sweltering haze,
A 30.9 temperature, for some, a daze.

Lahore's sun beats bright, a fiery blaze,
34.6 degrees, for passions, it sways.
But which one's best? The choice is not so clear,
Until you weigh, the weather's gentle cheer.

For if you seek, a calm and peaceful nest,
Aalborg's the place, where your heart finds rest.
But if you crave, the thrill of the heat,
Doha's the city, where your spirit takes a seat.

But then again, Lahore's charm is hard to resist,
A city of love, where hearts never miss.
It's hard to decide, which city's the best,
But one thing's for sure, each one passes the test.

So travel far, and wide, and explore,
Each of these cities, and their weather's score.
For in their differences, lies their unique charm,
And the best city, is the one that disarms.
