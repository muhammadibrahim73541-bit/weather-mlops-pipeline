**English Poem: Comparison of Cities**

Heavenly skies, a comparison we make,
In Lahore, Doha, Aalborg, each a unique stake,
Let's see who stands out in terms of weather so fair.

Aalborg, the mild, with a temperature high,
28.1 degrees on a summer's day, no need to sigh,
Gentle wind blows, 14 miles per hour's gentle pace,
A perfect haven in a world of busy space.

Doha, scorching hot, 43.4 degrees so bright,
A city that shines with a burning light,
Wind whispers at 14.6 miles per hour so fine,
But the heat is the real show, a fiery shine.

Lahore, the land of love, with 31.4 degrees so high,
Wind blows strong at 19.7 miles per hour, a romantic sigh,
But rain falls down, 18.2 inches, a surprise to see,
A city that dances with rain, so carefree.

Aalborg's the place for those who prefer the mild,
Doha's for the adventurous with a heart that's build,
Lahore's for the romantics, where love shines so bright,
So choose the city that suits you best, with all its delight.

**Urdu poem: Comparison of Cities**

ایک مہینے کے دن 28.1 درجے کی چمک

لیکن گرمی کی چمک، ایک جوش
