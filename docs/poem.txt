**English Poem**

Three cities so different and grand,
Aalborg, Doha, Lahore in a climate band.
Aalborg's chill, with 12.8°C high,
Rainy skies, with a gentle sigh.

Doha's heat, at 35.3°C, so bright,
Wind's whisper, through a scorching night.
No rain falls, in this desert sea,
A city born, to be carefree.

Lahore's fire, at 38.6°C, a flame,
Wind's gentle touch, in a parched terrain.
No rain falls, like Doha's sea,
A city's heart, in a desert spree.

So which one, to visit and stay?
Lahore might, be a traveler's way.
With food, culture, and history so grand,
Lahore's warm hearts, beckon to the land.

But Aalborg's charm, with rainy skies so blue,
Is perfect for some, a relaxing hue.
And Doha's heat, a traveler's dream,
For sun-seekers, a desert's golden theme.

So three cities stand, so different from each,
Choose Lahore, for a warm and vibrant reach.

**Urdu Poem (Transliterated from Urdu script)**

Tahar-e- Aalborg, Doha, Lahore main,
Tahar-e- jahani, khatir-e- badal hai.
Aalborg ki thandiyaan, 12.8°C ke sath,
Barish ke siyahi, ek hansi bhi nahi.

Doha ki garmi, 35.3°C ke saath,
Hawa ka shor, is tarah ke raat.
Barish ki kami, is khada-e-sehri mein,
Ek shahar ki shahar, khul jaye humein.

Lahore ki andhi, 38.6°C ki aag,
Hawa ki hawa, ek shoshan sehi.
Barish ki aabadi, Doha se bhi kharaab,
Ek shahar ki zameen, har shaq ko bhedkar.
Lahore ki shan, humari shahari shahar hai.
Kya dekhna hai, kya karna hai.
Aalborg ki shahar, har baar rain ho.
Lahore se, kuch ishaara ho.

Lahore ko choo, yahaan dekhna hai.
Khane, culture, aur itihaas ka sheen hai.

(Note: Transliteration might vary slightly as per different styles. The given translation is a neutral one, with focus on Urdu poetic style.)
