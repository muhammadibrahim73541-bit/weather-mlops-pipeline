**English Poem:**

Amidst the heat of Lahore's fiery night,
Doha's sun scorches with a burning light,
While Aalborg's chill begins to bite.

Three cities, diverse, yet all so fair,
Yet which one to choose, a traveler must share,
Their preferences, a tale to tell.

Lahore's vibrant streets, a treasure to see,
Doha's grandeur, a sight of majesty,
Aalborg's calm, a serenity.

Temperature high in Lahore and Doha's sway,
While Aalborg's cool breeze comes to stay,
Rainless skies, a common bond they share.

But if I had to choose, I'd say with glee,
Aalborg's charm, a place to be,
Cooler winds, a respite from the heat's grasp,
A place to unwind, and take a peaceful pass.
