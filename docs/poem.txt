**English Poem:**

In cities distant, far and wide,
Three metropolises do reside.
Lahore, Doha, Aalborg, each a sight,
Each with its own unique weather's might.

Lahore's heat is stifling, a burning flame,
Reaching 39.5°C, its temperature's aim.
Doha's wind whispers, a gentle caress,
Yet 38.6°C still is an infernal mess.

Aalborg's breeze is cool, a relief so sweet,
At 27.8°C, a respite to greet.
Aalborg's rain falls gently, a soothing stream,
While Lahore's drops are scarce, a mere dream.

If you prefer heat, then Doha's your place,
But for Lahore's fervor, a test of space.
But Aalborg's charm is hard to resist,
Its balance of temperatures, the ultimate hit.
