**English Poem: "Cities of Contrasts"**

In distant lands, three cities stand,
Aalborg's chill, Doha's burning sand,
Lahore's heat, a scorching flame,
Each unique, yet bearing a similar name.

Aalborg's breeze whispers through the night,
A gentle zephyr, a soothing sight,
But Doha's winds howl like a raging storm,
A furnace blast that leaves hearts forlorn.

Lahore's weather, a mix of both extremes,
A dry heat, with the occasional rain's themes,
But which city holds the key to delight?
Aalborg's calm, Doha's fiery night.

Let us choose Aalborg, where skies are bright,
A city of peace, where temperatures take flight,
No sweltering heat, no scorching sand to bear,
Just a gentle breeze, that's beyond compare.

So if you seek a city, free from strife,
Aalborg's your haven, for a peaceful life.

**Urdu Poem: "Tair-e-Tah-e-Mazameen"**

Aalborg, Doha, Lahore kuch hai,
Har ek nayi zameen, har ek nayi talee,
Aalborg kae sitam aaram aur shant hai,
Doha kae garmi aur Lahore kae bahaar hai.

Aalborg kae hawa ke zikr mein hum khush hain,
Doha kae hava to kheen hai, hawa ke garm zaban,
Lahore kae mauseeq aadha garmi hai aadha barsham hai.
Aur hum kis kis shahar kae baad kya hai.

Aalborg to hamari pasand hai, jo aaj kae shant hua hai,
Doha bhi hai shahar jiski taaza khaatir hawa hai,
Lekin Aalborg to shahar hai jahaan khushiyon ka mausam hai.

Agar teri pasand shahar seene walon kae nazar mein hoga,
Aalborg to tera shahar hai, jahaan aapko aaram milega.
Koi garmi, koi sauda, na koi aandhi, na koi barsham,
Aalborg to tera shahar hai, jahaan tum shant reho.
