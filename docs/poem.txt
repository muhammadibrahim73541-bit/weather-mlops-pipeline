**English Poem:**

In distant lands, three cities stand,
Lahore, Doha, and Aalborg's hand,
Each one unique, with stories to tell,
A tale of weather, as the winds dwell.

Lahore's heat, a burning flame,
Reaches high, but leaves one to wane,
Doha's desert, scorching sun's rays,
Beat down strong, in endless days.

Aalborg's shore, a gentler breeze,
Stirs calm waters, with a soothing squeeze,
A city by sea, its tranquility claims,
A peaceful haven, free from heat's sharp pains.

So if you're searching, for a place to be,
Where weather's kind, and calmness sets free,
Choose Aalborg's shore, where wind whispers low,
A haven from heat, in a world in tow.
