**English Poem:**

In Aalborg's chill of twenty-two,
Wind whispers softly, gentle and new.
No rain to dampen the summer's gleam,
A city of cool, a peaceful dream.

But then comes Doha's blazing heat,
Forty-five degrees, a sweltering beat.
The wind barely stirs, a distant sigh,
As sunbeams blaze, and temperatures fly.

And Lahore, in between, a city fair,
Thirty-two degrees, a mild, pleasant air.
The rain falls soft, a gentle stream,
A haven for those who seek a calm extreme.

So if you seek a balance just right,
With temperature, wind, and rain in sight.
Then let your heart be won to Lahore's way,
The city of perfect harmony today.
