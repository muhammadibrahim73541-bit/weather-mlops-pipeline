**English Poem:**

Three cities, far and wide apart,
Their weathers a stark contrast to heart.
In Aalborg's chill, winds do howl and sway,
While Doha's heat makes the sun shine all day.

In Lahore's streets, the scorching sun does beat,
Where temperatures soar to a sweltering heat.
Yet, amidst this heat, the beauty does shine,
A vibrant city, where hearts and souls entwine.

But, if I had to choose, I'd pick a place,
Aalborg's calm and beauty, leave the heat and pace.
Where the North Sea's breeze does gently blow,
Aalborg's the haven, where I'd love to go.

For in its quiet streets, the heart finds peace,
A place to unwind, and the world to release.
So, if you're torn between heat and cold,
Choose Aalborg, where love and peace do unfold.
