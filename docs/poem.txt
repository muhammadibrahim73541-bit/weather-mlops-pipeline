**English Poem**

Cities of the world, so diverse and wide,
Their climates, a tale to abide.

Aalborg, a chill in the air,
20.5°C, with wind that blows there.
Rainy days few, a gentle touch,
A place for rest, a quiet clutch.

Doha, a heat that burns the skin,
43.3°C, the temperature within.
No rain to soothe, a burning sun,
A place for those who dare to have won.

Lahore, a mix of warmth and rain,
29.9°C, with wind that whispers and pains.
Rain 32.8, a monsoon's sway,
A city of life, in a vibrant way.

Which one's the best, you might ask,
A question of taste, a personal task.
But if I had to choose, I'd say,
Lahore's the place, where life comes to stay.

The temperature's just right, the rain's a delight,
The people are warm, in this city's sight.
So if you ever get the chance to go,
Choose Lahore, and let its beauty grow.
