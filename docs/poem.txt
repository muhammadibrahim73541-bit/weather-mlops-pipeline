**English Poem:**

In lands of sun and wind so fair,
Three cities stand with temperature to share.
Lahore's heat is fiery, a scorching flame,
Doha's breeze is warm, with temperatures the same.

Aalborg's chill is crisp and cold as stone,
A winter's breeze that cuts through bone to bone.
Rain falls gently there, with steady pace,
But not a drop falls in Doha's arid space.

Wind whispers secrets in each city's ear,
Their tales of climate, unique and rare.
Lahore's desert winds that howl with pain,
Doha's calm seas that bring a soothing rain.

Aalborg's frosty mornings, dark and gray,
Doha's sun-kissed afternoons in a warm way.
But if I had to choose, I'd say with glee,
Aalborg's temperate charm is the best for me.

The balance of seasons, a perfect blend,
Lahore's heat, Doha's calm, all to amend.

البوگ کا خفیف اور سرما، بھاپی سے ہوا بہتی ہے
