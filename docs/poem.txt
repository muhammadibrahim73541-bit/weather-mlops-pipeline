**English Poem: "Cities of Contrast"**

In distant lands, three cities stand,
Their climates unique, a traveler's plan.
Aalborg, cool and gentle as a breeze,
Lahore and Doha, hot and sweltering as the trees.

In Aalborg's rain, the streets come alive,
The 13.7 winds whisper, a soothing jive.
The temperature's pleasant, 19.2 degrees high,
A perfect refuge, from a world passing by.

But Doha's heat, a scorching flame,
41.5°C, a fiery blaze with no fame.
Rain, a rare sight, a precious drop of dew,
A reminder of water's value, anew.

Lahore's heat, an unrelenting test,
41.9°C, a furnace that never finds rest.
No rain, no mercy, a burning sun's rays,
A challenge to survive, in the sweltering haze.

But if I were to choose, a city so fine,
I'd choose Aalborg, with its gentle design.
The climate fair, the scenery so grand,
Aalborg's the perfect, haven for this traveler's land.

**Urdu Poem: "Tarin Qabrasti" (Cities of Contrast)**

Tairin qabrasti, doosron ki dastaan,
Dilchiya ki dharti, mein taron ki aas.
Alborg: koi thandi hai, koi shabnam,
Lahore, Doha: garm hai, koi hawa kam.

Alborg ki bartan, barshaon ki gudi,
Sipahi ki awaaz, dastaanon ki bhavan.
Tampar 19.2: josh-e-dil mein,
Lahour aur Doha: chammak ki ghum.

Daha ki garmi, duniya ki chidiye,
41.5°C: aag ki khabar, kuchh bhi nahin.
Barsha: gair-kabhi, kam-zubaan,
Paani ki kasar, duniya hai aatishi galiyan.

Lahore ki garmi, jis ki thandi nahin,
41.9°C ki aag: hamesha khali galiyan.
Koi barsha, koi gair-kam-kam,
Sulat-e-junoon: kuchh na khali galiyan.

Lekin agar main chun sakon,
Ek shahar to aapko kyun nahin doong.
Main Alborg ko, apni dastaan mein,
Lekhon ga: shahar-e-kashish, Alborg ki taran.
